---
title: Elevation
order: 3
summary: Five levels, each a shadow paired with a surface color, used through role tokens such as `--dui-elevation-popover`.
principle: elevation
tokens:
  - semantic: Elevation knobs
  - semantic: Elevation
  - semantic: Elevation by role
  - primitives: Shadow geometry
---
Components use the role tokens, never the level numbers, so a theme can move a role to another level.
In light schemes the shadow grows with the level; in dark schemes the surface gets lighter instead.
