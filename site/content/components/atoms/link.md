---
usage:
  - For dense UI, such as table cells, footers and metadata, where many accent-colored links would be noisy.
  - Visited links keep the surrounding color too.
---
- The underline stays, so color is never the only cue ([WCAG 1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)).
- The link takes the surrounding text color, which already has 4.5:1 contrast.
