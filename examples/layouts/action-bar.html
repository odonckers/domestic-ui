Form page with sticky action bar

A long form whose Save and Cancel buttons stay in view, and never hide the field that has focus.

API: data-action-bar on a <footer> that is the last child of the <form>. Source: css/layout/action-bar.css.

The bar sticks to the bottom of the window while the form scrolls and comes to rest after the last field. Every ancestor of the form reserves a scroll-padding as tall as the bar, so Tab scrolls a field under the bar clear of it (WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured). At 320px the buttons wrap onto a second row.
