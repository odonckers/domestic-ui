Split

Two regions side by side at a set ratio, stacked when the wrapper is narrow.

API: data-split="[1:1 | 1:2 | 2:1]" on a wrapper of exactly two regions; empty means equal halves. Source: css/layout/split.css.

The regions keep their source order at every width. The divider is fixed: a draggable one would follow the APG Window Splitter pattern and is out of scope; see the split view explainer.
