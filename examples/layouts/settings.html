Settings page

Settings sections listed beside the forms that edit them: a composition of list-detail, form sections and the action bar.

No new API: data-list-detail places a <nav> of in-page links beside a pane of data-form-sections, with a data-action-bar at the end of the form. When side by side the pane scrolls on its own and the action bar sticks to its bottom edge; the links scroll the pane to each section. When each section is its own URL, link to it instead and mark the current one with aria-current="page".
