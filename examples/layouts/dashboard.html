Dashboard

A grid of headed tiles that reflows from one column on a phone to as many as fit on a wide screen.

API: data-dashboard on the wrapper; data-dashboard="wide" on a tile that spans two columns when they fit, data-dashboard="full" on a tile that spans the row. Tiles are <section> elements named by their heading. Source: css/layout/dashboard.css.
