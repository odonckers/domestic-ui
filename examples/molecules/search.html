Search field

The <search> landmark around a GET form. No JavaScript.

API: no attribute: <search> > <form> with a labelled <input type="search"> and a submit button. Source: css/components/molecules/search.css.

<search> is a landmark screen reader users can jump to. The input always has a <label>; when there is no room for it, the label is visually hidden and the button still says "Search".
