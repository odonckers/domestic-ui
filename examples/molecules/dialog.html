Dialog and drawer

The native <dialog>, opened with invoker commands, plus a drawer variant.

API: data-dialog and data-dialog="drawer [start | end]" on <dialog>. Source: css/components/molecules/dialog.css.

command="show-modal" opens a modal dialog with no script: the browser makes the page inert, moves focus in, closes on Esc and returns focus to the button (APG Dialog (Modal)). closedby="any" also closes it on a click outside. The drawer is the same element at an edge of the viewport; see the drawer explainer.
