Alert and toast

Inline alerts with a tone, live regions that announce changes, and toasts that wait for you.

API: data-alert="info | success | warning | danger"; role="status" or role="alert" for live regions; <dui-toaster data-alert="toasts" role="status"> for toasts. Source: css/components/molecules/alerts.css.

Each tone starts with a word ("Warning:") in the markup and has an icon, so color is not the only cue. A live region must be in the page before its text changes, or screen readers may stay silent (WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages).

Inline alerts

Present on load, so they need no live region role.

Info: Maintenance is planned for Sunday at 02:00.

Success: Your changes were published.

Warning: Your trial ends in 3 days.

Choose a plan to keep your projects.

Error: The file could not be uploaded. Try a file under 10 MB.

<div class="stack">
  <div data-alert="info">
    <p><strong>Info:</strong> Maintenance is planned for Sunday at 02:00.</p>
  </div>
  <div data-alert="success">
    <p><strong>Success:</strong> Your changes were published.</p>
  </div>
  <div data-alert="warning">
    <p><strong>Warning:</strong> Your trial ends in 3 days.</p>
    <p><a href="#inline-title">Choose a plan</a> to keep your projects.</p>
  </div>
  <div data-alert="danger">
    <p><strong>Error:</strong> The file could not be uploaded. Try a file under 10 MB.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Live regions

Both regions are empty on load. The buttons put text into them: the status is read when the screen reader is idle, the alert interrupts. 

<div class="cluster">
  <button type="button" data-announce="live-status">Save settings</button>
  <button type="button" data-announce="live-alert">Send without a subject</button>
</div>
<div id="live-status" role="status"></div>
<div id="live-alert" role="alert"></div>

Toasts

The toast region sits at the bottom inline-end corner and is in the page from the start. Messages stay until you dismiss them; Tab reaches each Dismiss button. 

<div class="cluster">
  <button type="button" data-toast="success">Copy link</button>
  <button type="button" data-toast="info">Start export</button>
  <button type="button" data-toast="danger">Sync now</button>
</div>
