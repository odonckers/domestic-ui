Tabs

APG tabs from readable markup: a <nav> of in-page links and the sections they point at.

API: <dui-tabs> around a <nav aria-label> of <a href="#id"> links and <section id> panels; activation="manual" optional; aria-current="true" on a link selects it on load. Source: css/components/molecules/tabs.css.

<dui-tabs> (web-components/dui-tabs.js) adds the APG Tabs roles, states and keys. With JavaScript off, every section shows with its heading and the links jump to them. Selection follows focus (automatic activation) unless you set activation="manual".
