Toolbar

A labelled group of controls with one Tab stop and arrow keys between them.

API: role="toolbar" and aria-label on <dui-toolbar>; aria-orientation="vertical" optional; aria-pressed on toggles. Source: css/components/molecules/toolbar.css.

<dui-toolbar> (web-components/dui-toolbar.js) adds the roving tabindex of the APG Toolbar. Without JavaScript every button stays a normal Tab stop. Pressed toggles have a tint, accent text and a doubled border.

Text formatting

 
<dui-toolbar role="toolbar" aria-label="Text formatting">
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="true">Bold</button>
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="false">Italic</button>
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="false">Underline</button>
  <hr>
  <button type="button">Insert link</button>
  <button type="button">Clear formatting</button>
</dui-toolbar>

Vertical

Up Arrow and Down Arrow move between the controls.

<dui-toolbar role="toolbar" aria-label="Drawing tools" aria-orientation="vertical">
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="true">Select</button>
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="false">Pen</button>
  <button type="button" aria-pressed="false">Eraser</button>
</dui-toolbar>
