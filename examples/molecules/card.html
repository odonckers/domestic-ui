Card

A self-contained summary that links to more, with a whole-card link that keeps the heading as its name.

API: data-card and data-card="link" on <article>. Source: css/components/molecules/card.css.

There is no card element or APG pattern; the contract is in the card explainer. In a clickable card the heading's link covers the card with a pseudo-element, so its accessible name is just the heading, and footer actions stay clickable above it (Inclusive Components: Cards).
