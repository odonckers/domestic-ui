Search dialog

A command palette: a modal <dialog> holding a combobox of pages and sections, opened from a Search button or with Cmd+K or Ctrl+K.

API: none of its own. data-dialog on <dialog> (dialog.css) and <dui-combobox> (combobox.css), plus a few lines of page script.

It composes the APG Dialog (Modal) and APG Combobox patterns. The browser makes the page inert, moves focus to the field (autofocus) and returns it on close. The button is hidden until the script that fills the results runs. See the search dialog explainer.
