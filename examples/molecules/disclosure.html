Disclosure and accordion

Show and hide content with <details> and <summary>. No JavaScript.

API: data-disclosure="accordion" on a wrapper, data-disclosure="plain" on a <details>, native name and open. Source: css/components/molecules/disclosure.css.

<summary> is exposed as a button with an expanded state, which is the APG Disclosure pattern with no ARIA to write. Give several <details> the same name and opening one closes the others: an exclusive accordion. Unlike the APG Accordion example, the headers are not headings and there is no arrow-key movement between them (both optional in the APG); Tab moves from one summary to the next.
