Motion

Every transition reads its duration and easing from --dui-motion-* tokens. --dui-motion-scale multiplies every duration, and --dui-motion-movement turns slides, lifts and zooms on (1) or off (0). With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, or inside data-motion="reduced", movement stops and fades stay. Inside data-motion="none" nothing transitions. Focus rings, validation errors, alert text and loading states always change at once. Source: css/tokens/semantic.css.

Fade: dialog

Fades and lifts by --dui-motion-distance over --dui-motion-duration-normal; the backdrop fades with it.

Fade in, fade out

Enters with --dui-motion-easing-enter and leaves with --dui-motion-easing-exit.

Slide: drawer

Slides from the inline-end edge over --dui-motion-duration-slow. With reduced motion it fades in place.

Drawer

The off-screen offset is 100% times --dui-motion-movement.

Scale: popover and menu

Grows from --dui-motion-zoom while it fades, over --dui-motion-duration-fast.

Grows and fades in.

Color and state: buttons, links, switch

Colors fade over --dui-motion-duration-fast with --dui-motion-easing-standard. The switch thumb slides; with reduced motion it jumps and the color still fades.

Toast

A new toast fades in and lifts by --dui-motion-distance. Its text is announced at once; only the box moves.

Motion scale

--dui-motion-scale: 3 on a scope makes everything inside three times slower. Set it on :root for the page, or on an element with data-motion (empty) or data-theme, where the derived durations are re-declared.

Three times the normal duration.

Reduced and no motion

data-motion="reduced" behaves like prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: fades and color changes stay, movement goes. data-motion="none" sets every duration to 0s.

View transition (progressive enhancement)

Where document.startViewTransition() exists, reversing the list animates each item to its new place; elsewhere the list simply changes. The base layer gives view transitions the motion tokens, so they follow --dui-motion-scale, reduced motion and data-motion on the root like everything else.
