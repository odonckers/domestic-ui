Elevation

Five levels, --dui-elevation-0 to --dui-elevation-4, each a shadow paired with a surface (--dui-elevation-0-surface to --dui-elevation-4-surface). In the light scheme the shadow does the work. In the dark scheme shadows barely show, so each level also mixes a little more of the text color into its surface. Source: css/tokens/semantic.css.

Levels in light and dark

The same five swatches, forced to each scheme with data-color-scheme. Text, muted text and links meet 4.5:1 on every surface.

By role

Components use the role names, never the numbers: --dui-elevation-raised for cards, --dui-elevation-overlay for toasts, --dui-elevation-popover for popovers, menus and tooltips, --dui-elevation-dialog for dialogs and drawers, each with a matching -surface token.

Forced colors

In forced colors mode (Windows High Contrast and similar) browsers drop box-shadow and replace surface colors with the system Canvas. Every elevated component keeps a border, drawn in CanvasText under @media (forced-colors: active), so each layer still has a visible edge. The swatches above do the same.
