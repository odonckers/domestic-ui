Text helpers

Three presentation helpers for prose: a lead paragraph, small text and muted text.

API: data-text="lead | small | muted". Source: css/components/atoms/text.css.

They change size or color only. Meaning still comes from HTML: use <small> for side comments and <strong> for importance. Code and keyboard input are already styled by the base layer (base elements).

One step up the type scale, for the paragraph that opens a page or section.
