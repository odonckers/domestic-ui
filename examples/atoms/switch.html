Switch

An on/off control: a native checkbox with role="switch".

API: role="switch" on <input type="checkbox">. Source: css/components/atoms/switch.css.

This follows the APG Switch pattern (its HTML checkbox example). The checkbox gives focus, Space to toggle and form submission; the role makes screen readers say "on" and "off". The label stays the same in both states. The state shows by the thumb's position and color and the track fill, not by color alone, and uses system colors in forced-colors mode.
