Badge

A short inline status label. Static text, not interactive.

API: data-badge="neutral | accent | success | warning | danger". Source: css/components/atoms/badge.css.

The words carry the meaning; the tone only supports it (WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color). Text on every tone meets 4.5:1 in light and dark. If a badge must do something, wrap it in a real <button> or <a href>.
