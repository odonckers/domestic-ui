Data table

Records in a native <table> with a caption and row headers, plus sorting, selection, empty and loading states.

API: data-table on the <table> (data-table="sticky" for a sticky header), and on cells data-table="number" for numeric columns and data-table="sortable" for sortable headers. Source: css/components/organisms/data-table.css.

Sorting and selection come from <dui-table> (web-components/dui-table.js), which follows the APG Sortable Table example. With JavaScript off the headers are plain text, the rows keep the server's order, and the checkboxes are ordinary form controls.

Sortable columns

Each sortable header becomes a button. The sorted column has aria-sort; the arrow is hidden from screen readers. Totals sort as numbers and dates by their datetime.

Selection and bulk actions

Each checkbox is named after its row. The checkbox in the header selects every row and is mixed when some are selected; the count is a status message, and the bulk actions are enabled only while rows are selected.

Loading state

While rows load the table has aria-busy="true" and skeleton rows that are hidden from screen readers; a status message says what is happening. The shimmer stops under reduced motion and data-motion="none".

Wide table with a sticky header

In data-scroll="block inline" the table scrolls both ways inside a box at most as tall as the viewport, and with data-table="sticky" its header stays at the top of that box. The page itself never scrolls sideways, even at 320 pixels.
