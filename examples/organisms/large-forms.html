Large forms

A long form split into headed sections: one column on narrow screens, the heading beside the fields on wide ones.

API: data-form-sections on the <form>; each child <section> holds a <header> (heading and description) and one body element of fields. Source: css/components/organisms/large-forms.css.

From 48rem of the form's own width (a container query) each section puts its heading and description beside its fields, and the fields form a grid of as many columns as fit. Text areas, groups and notes take the whole row. Radio buttons and other related controls stay in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. The buttons are in a sticky action bar.
