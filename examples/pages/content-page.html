Shipping your first order

From a new order to a parcel on its way, in four steps.

Pick and pack

Open the order, check each line against the shelf, and scan the items into the parcel. Northwind warns you when a count does not match.

Choose a carrier

Rates from your connected carriers appear side by side. The cheapest one that meets the promised date is selected for you.

Standard

Three to five working days. Tracking included.

Express

Next working day when packed before 3 PM.

Collect

The customer picks the parcel up from your warehouse.

Print and ship

Print the label and packing slip together, then mark the order as shipped. The customer gets the tracking link by email.
