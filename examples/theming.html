Theming

A theme is an override of semantic tokens on :root or on a scoped element. No build step, no extra classes on the content. The right-hand panel below uses data-theme="forest" and this CSS, which is the only difference between the two panels: 

[data-theme="forest"] {
  --dui-color-surface: light-dark(#f7f5ef, #141a16);
  --dui-color-surface-raised: light-dark(#eeeadf, #1d2620);
  --dui-color-text: light-dark(#1d2a22, #e4ebe3);
  --dui-color-border: light-dark(#d8d1bf, #34433a);
  --dui-color-accent: light-dark(#2f6b3f, #7fcf95);
  --dui-color-accent-contrast: light-dark(#fff, #0f1a13);
  --dui-font-heading: charter, "Bitstream Charter", "Sitka Text", cambria, serif;
  --dui-radius-control: 0.125rem;
  --dui-radius-surface: 0.25rem;
}

Links, focus rings, button hover colors and the tinted badge backgrounds follow --dui-color-accent and --dui-color-surface automatically, because those tokens are re-derived on every [data-theme] scope. The atoms in each panel (badges, a field with a hint and an error, a switch and button variants) pick up the theme with no extra CSS. Both themes follow your system light or dark preference through light-dark().
